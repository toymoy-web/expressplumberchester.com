A Small Borough Built Around the Refinery

Marcus Hook is one of the smallest boroughs in our service area, just over one square mile with about 2,400 residents, incorporated in 1892 as the Sunoco refinery (opened 1901) grew into the borough's defining industry for the next century. Housing here is dense and modest, built for refinery and shipyard workers through the early-to-mid 1900s, clustered around the business district at 10th and Market Street and landmarks like Mickey Vernon Park and Market Square Memorial Park along the Delaware River.

That compact, century-old housing stock means plumbing systems here have seen decades of use in a small footprint, with many homes still on original supply and drain lines installed when the borough was still a company town in every practical sense.

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

  • Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Turn off electricity to any area with standing water before touching outlets or appliances there.
  • Take a few photos of the damage before cleanup starts, for a homeowners insurance claim.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line; a technician will confirm arrival timing on the phone.

Signs Your Marcus Hook Home Needs a Plumber Now

  • A water bill that's climbed with no change in usage, a common sign of a hidden leak in older supply lines.
  • A drain that clogs repeatedly near 10th Street or the older sections close to Market Square.
  • No hot water or a water heater making unusual noise.
  • Sewage odor near a floor drain or cleanout.
  • Low water pressure at one fixture while the rest of the house is normal.
