Where Park Place Sits in Chester

Park Place is a neighborhood of the City of Chester according to City-Data, whose profile lists ZIP codes 19013 and 19015. The streets it names in the area include Edgmont Ave (State Route 352), W 22nd St, W 23rd St, W 24th St, Crozer St, Upland Ave, Howard St and Park Ave. No official boundary is published, so we use those streets only as a general guide.

The profile gives a mixed picture of housing. Detached houses are about a quarter of units and townhouses or other attached homes are about two thirds. By its build-year chart, the largest group of homes, 385 of roughly 980, dates from 1939 or earlier, followed by 179 from the 1940s and 142 from the 1950s. These are data-site estimates and not city records.

Walk Score lists a neighborhood called Edgemont Park in Chester as well. We could not verify where it ends and Park Place begins, so we do not separate the two. If your address is near W 21st through W 24th Street, call us and we will take it from there.

Park Place addresses fall under Chester ZIP 19013 and, per City-Data, 19015, a ZIP also used by Upland, which borders Chester. You can also see our main Chester plumbing page and our page for nearby Upland.

Plumbing Notes for Mixed Housing

This section is general advice. When one neighborhood holds detached houses, twins and rows from several decades, two neighbors can have very different plumbing even on the same block. A good technician checks what is actually in your house and does not guess from the street name or the year.

That matters most for water heaters and supply lines. Ask what type you have, where it vents and where the shutoff valves are before any work is quoted.

  • Note which fixtures are affected and when the problem started.
  • Look for the water heater label and write down the model and age if it is visible.
  • Find the main shutoff valve so you can close it fast.
  • Take photos of any wet spots for your own records and for insurance.
