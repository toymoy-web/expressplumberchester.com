If You Smell Gas: Safety Steps First

  • Leave the house immediately. Do not stay inside to investigate the source.
  • Do not flip light switches, use your phone inside, or do anything that could create a spark on your way out.
  • Do not attempt to locate or fix the leak yourself.
  • Once safely outside, call our 24/7 emergency line or your gas utility's emergency number.
  • Do not re-enter the house until a technician or the gas utility confirms it's safe.

Common Gas Line Calls

Suspected Leak

A rotten-egg smell near a stove, water heater, or gas meter is investigated and repaired immediately as an emergency call.

New Appliance Connection

A new gas range, dryer, or outdoor fire feature needs a properly sized line run and connected to code, not just tapped into the nearest existing line.

Line Relocation

A kitchen remodel or addition sometimes requires moving an existing gas line, which needs the same permitting and inspection as new installation.

Licensed Work, Not a DIY Project

Gas line work in Chester requires a licensed plumber and, in most cases, a permit and municipal inspection before the line goes into service, since Pennsylvania handles this at the municipal level rather than through a single statewide licensing system.

Installation follows the National Fuel Gas Code, the standard governing gas piping sizing, materials, and safety clearances used across the plumbing trade, which is why a line has to be sized correctly for the specific appliance rather than just connected to whatever pipe happens to be nearby. Rowhomes along Lamokin Street and other blocks dating to Chester's early-1900s industrial boom are typical examples.

A pressure test is standard practice after any new gas line installation or repair, confirming there are no leaks anywhere along the new or repaired section before an appliance is connected and the gas turned back on.

Older Chester Buildings and Gas Line Age

Many gas lines in Chester's older housing stock were installed decades before today's typical appliance lineup existed, sized for a smaller gas load than a modern range, tankless water heater, or outdoor grill actually draws. Connecting a new high-demand appliance to an undersized original line without checking capacity first is a real, avoidable way to end up with a line that can't keep pace once more than one appliance runs at the same time.

Black iron pipe, the material used in most of Chester's older gas lines, holds up well over decades but is still inspected for corrosion at fittings and joints during any service call. A gas meter upgrade is occasionally needed alongside a larger appliance addition too, since the meter itself has a maximum capacity separate from the line running to the appliance, and utility coordination for that upgrade is arranged as part of the project.

Emergency Signs of a Gas Line Problem

  • A rotten-egg or sulfur smell near an appliance, the meter, or along a wall.
  • A hissing sound near a gas line or connection.
  • Dead or discolored vegetation in a patch above an underground gas line.
  • An appliance's pilot light that won't stay lit despite repeated attempts.
  • Unusually high gas bills with no change in appliance usage.

Gas Leak Emergency: Safety Before Repair

If you smell gas, leave the building immediately with everyone inside. Do not use light switches, phones, or anything that can spark inside the house. Call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not go back in until the utility or fire department says it is safe.

Once the utility has made the area safe, call our 24/7 line. Gas piping repairs must be done by a licensed plumber, and we test the line for leaks before it is put back in service.

Gas Line Installation and Inspection

We also install new gas lines for stoves, dryers, water heaters, and fireplaces, and inspect existing piping before an appliance change. Moving from a natural gas appliance to propane, or the other way, involves different regulators and orifices, so ask before you buy the appliance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I smell gas in my house?

Leave immediately without flipping switches or using anything that could spark, then call our 24/7 line or your gas utility's emergency number from outside. Do not go back in until a technician confirms it's safe.

Can I install a gas line myself for a new stove or dryer?

No. Gas line work requires a licensed plumber and, in most cases, a permit and inspection in Chester. This isn't a DIY project given the safety risk involved.

How do I know if my dead grass patch is from a gas leak?

Dead or unusually discolored vegetation directly above a gas line's path, especially without an obvious other cause like drought, is worth having checked. It's one of several signs, alongside smell or a hissing sound, that point to an underground leak.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency gas line repair?

Yes. A suspected gas leak is treated as an emergency call around the clock, since it's a safety issue that can't wait for regular scheduling.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the building right away, do not use switches or phones inside, and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Call us after the utility has made the area safe.

Can you convert an appliance from natural gas to propane?

Conversion involves different regulators and orifices and depends on the appliance. Call (610) 672-7601 and we will tell you what is involved before you buy.
