Brookhaven's Housing Stock: Colonial Rowhomes to Postwar Ranches

Brookhaven was incorporated as a borough in 1945, but its oldest housing predates that by a century: colonial-era rowhomes near Chester Creek and Ridley Creek, some dating to the 1850s, sit alongside the postwar ranches and townhomes built as the borough grew from a dairy-and-mill economy into a suburban community of roughly 8,300 people.

That mix matters for plumbing because a home's pipe material tracks its construction era almost exactly: the older rowhomes near the creeks are more likely to still run original galvanized supply lines, while the postwar sections lean toward copper installed when those homes were built.

The borough's name for its oldest section, Green Ridge, and the neighboring Sackville area sit closest to both creek boundaries, which is also where basements and crawlspaces run dampest, a real factor in sump pump reliability and slab-adjacent leak calls in that part of town.
