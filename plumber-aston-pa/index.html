Aston Township's Neighborhoods

Aston Township is one of Delaware County's oldest municipal districts, settled in 1682 and incorporated in 1688, but its housing is largely newer and more suburban than Chester's dense rowhome core: Aston is home to roughly 16,800 residents spread across 5.8 square miles, a much lower density than Chester proper. The Village Green census-designated place, along with the Concord Road corridor near the historic Seven Stars Inn at Five Points, anchors the township's older section, while newer development sits closer to Neumann University.

That lower-density, more suburban housing pattern is the reason Aston's call mix looks different from Chester's: fewer original galvanized supply-line leaks, more standard water heater, drain, and fixture work typical of mid-to-late-20th-century single-family construction.

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

  • Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Turn off electricity to any area with standing water before touching outlets or appliances there.
  • Take a few photos of the damage before cleanup starts, for a homeowners insurance claim.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line; a technician will confirm arrival timing on the phone.

Signs Your Aston Home Needs a Plumber Now

  • No hot water or a water heater nearing 10 years old.
  • A slow or clogged drain in a kitchen or bathroom.
  • A toilet that runs continuously or rocks slightly when you sit down.
  • A sump pump that hasn't been tested since it was installed.
  • Low water pressure at one specific fixture.
