Plumbing Services in Chester, PA

Chester Express Plumbing covers the full range of residential plumbing work in Chester and nearby Delaware County, from routine repairs to 24/7 emergencies.

Every service below follows the same upfront-pricing standard: a flat quote confirmed before any repair work begins, and same-day availability, with 24/7 emergency dispatch for burst pipes, no hot water, backed-up drains, and active leaks. Chester's older rowhome and twin housing stock, much of it built during the city's early-1900s industrial boom, means several of these services (leak detection, sewer line repair, and burst pipe repair especially) come up more often here than in newer construction elsewhere in Delaware County. If you are not sure which service matches your problem, call and describe what is happening; a technician will point you to the right page and the right fix rather than guessing over the phone. Every listed service is also reachable from the top of this page and from the phone number in the header, so a call placed from any device on the site reaches the same dispatch line.

What to Do Before You Call in a Plumbing Emergency

  • Know where your home's main water shutoff valve is located before you actually need it.
  • Shut off that valve first for any burst pipe, major leak, or water damage in progress.
  • For a suspected gas smell, leave the house and call from outside rather than investigating indoors.
  • Call and describe what you're seeing; a technician will route you to the right service and, if needed, the right emergency line.
