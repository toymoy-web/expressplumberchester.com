A Small Refinery Town on the Delaware

Trainer is a borough of just under 2,000 people on the Delaware River, directly east of our Chester coverage area and bordered by Marcus Hook to the southwest and Upper Chichester Township to the north. It was incorporated as its own borough in 1919, carved out of Lower Chichester Township and named for David Trainer Sr., owner of the old Linwood Mills textile operation here. Today the riverfront is still industrial, home to the Trainer Refinery, part of the Port of Chester's heavily industrialized shoreline.

Away from the refinery itself, Trainer's residential streets are largely early-to-mid 1900s construction built for mill and refinery workers, with the same kind of aging supply lines and exposed basement pipe runs that drive cold-weather emergency calls across this stretch of the river.

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

  • Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Turn off electricity to any area with standing water before touching outlets or appliances there.
  • Take a few photos of the damage before cleanup starts, for a homeowners insurance claim.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line. A technician will confirm arrival timing on the phone.

Winterizing Older Trainer Homes

  • Insulate exposed pipes in basements and crawlspaces before the first hard freeze each year.
  • Know where your main shutoff valve is before you need it in an emergency.
  • Let faucets drip slightly during extreme cold to relieve pressure in exposed lines.
  • Disconnect and drain outdoor hoses and spigots before winter.

Industrial Riverfront, Residential Side Streets
