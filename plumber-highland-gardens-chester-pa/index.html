What Public Sources Say About Highland Gardens

Neighborhoods.com describes Highland Gardens as a neighborhood in Chester, Pennsylvania, lists its age as 1943 and gives its housing types as attached homes and single-family homes. Its current listings on that page are on Bethel Rd, Honan St and Smithers St, which are listing addresses and not a boundary.

The Delaware County Heritage Commission's list of National Register resources (October 2021) includes a Highland Gardens Historic District under Chester City, dated 1942 and marked as an eligible district. We read that to mean it was identified as eligible for the National Register. We could not confirm that it is actually listed, and the list gives no address or boundary for it.

Because no public source gives exact streets, we do not either. If your home is in Highland Gardens or close to it, we serve you the same as the rest of Chester.

Highland Gardens is within the City of Chester, whose main ZIP is 19013. Neighborhoods.com does not state a ZIP for it. You can also see our main Chester plumbing page and our page for nearby Upland.

Plumbing Notes for Homes From the 1940s

This section is general advice. A house built around the same decade as its neighbors may still have had its plumbing changed since then. We do not assume that old homes have any particular pipe material. A technician confirms what is installed by looking.

If you are planning a bathroom or kitchen remodel, ask for the plumbing to be assessed first. Knowing where supply lines, shutoffs and drains run helps you plan the work and the budget. An eligible historic district is not the same as a listed one, so check with the city about any exterior work and not with us.

  • Close the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Turn off power to any area with standing water before you touch outlets.
  • Move valuables and photograph the damage.
  • Call our 24/7 line and describe where the water is coming from.

Is Highland Gardens an official Chester neighborhood?

Neighborhoods.com lists it as one, and the county heritage list includes a Highland Gardens Historic District in Chester as an eligible district. No public source we found gives exact boundaries.
