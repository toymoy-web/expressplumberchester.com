Where Sun Village Sits in Chester

Sun Village is a section of the City of Chester. Wikipedia's entry for Chester says U.S. Route 13 follows Morton Avenue through what it calls the city's Sun Village section. City-Data's neighborhood profile lists the area under ZIP codes 19013 and 19022, and shows I-95, the Baltimore and Ohio railroad line and Ridley Creek among the features in it, along with streets such as E 9th St, E 10th St, Chester Pike, Ridley Ave and Sun Dr.

Public sources do not publish an official Sun Village boundary, so we do not draw one here. If you are not sure whether your address counts as Sun Village or a neighboring part of Chester, it makes no difference to service. We cover all of Chester.

The same City-Data profile describes townhouse and other attached homes as the large majority of units in Sun Village. That is a data-site estimate and not a city record. Data sites also list Sun Hill as its own neighborhood, and the two profiles share several street names, including Hancock St, Johnson St and Terrill St, so we treat them as neighbors that overlap on the map.

City-Data lists Chester ZIP 19013 for Sun Village and also 19022, the ZIP of Eddystone, where we have a page of its own. You can also see our main Chester plumbing page and our page for nearby Eddystone.

Plumbing Notes for Attached Homes

This section is general plumbing advice and not a claim about any one house. In a home that shares walls with its neighbors, a leak can show up in the next house before it shows up in yours. If you see a stain or hear running water, tell the neighbor early.

  • Find your main water shutoff valve and turn it once a year so you know it still closes.
  • Watch the basement floor drain. Slow drains at several fixtures at once point to the main drain and not to one sink.
  • Keep grease, wipes and paper towels out of drains and toilets.
  • If a pipe leaks into a shared wall, photograph it and call right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover Sun Village for emergencies?

Yes. Our 24/7 emergency line covers the whole City of Chester, including Sun Village. Call to confirm arrival timing for your address.

Is Sun Village the same as Sun Hill?

Data sites list them as separate neighborhoods, and their profiles share some streets. No official boundary is published, so we go by your address. We serve both.

What if the leak is in a wall I share with a neighbor?

A technician can look at your side and tell you what they find. Work inside a neighbor's home needs that owner's permission.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely. Turn off power to any area with standing water, then call our 24/7 line.
