What to Do in the First Five Minutes

  • Shut off the main water valve, usually in the basement or a front crawlspace in Chester's older rowhomes.
  • Turn off electricity to any area with standing water before touching outlets or appliances there.
  • Move valuables and furniture away from the affected area if it is safe to do so.
  • Take a few photos of the damage for a homeowners insurance claim before cleanup starts.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line; a technician will confirm arrival timing on the phone.
2

Why Burst Pipes Are Common in Chester's Older Homes

A cold snap is the most frequent cause we see: an exposed or under-insulated line in an older basement, crawlspace, or exterior wall freezes, expands, and cracks, then fails once things thaw and pressure returns. Chester's rowhomes and twins, many built well before modern insulation standards, have more of these exposed runs than newer construction elsewhere in the county. This same vintage shows up across Chester, from the Sun Village neighborhood to the streets near Widener University.

The second common cause is simple age: original galvanized supply lines that have corroded from the inside for decades eventually fail at a weak point, often at a joint or an elbow, with no cold weather involved at all.

How We Repair a Burst Pipe

Emergency Shutoff

The first step on arrival is confirming the water is fully off at the source, not just at the visible break, to stop any continued flow.

Section Repair or Replacement

The damaged section is cut out and replaced; if the surrounding pipe shows similar corrosion, replacing a longer run at once can prevent a repeat call.

Pressure Test

Before the technician leaves, the repaired section is pressure tested to confirm there are no other weak points nearby that could fail next.

Burst Pipe Repair vs. Water Damage Cleanup

Fixing the pipe itself, stopping the leak and repairing the break, is plumbing repair work and is priced and handled the way any other repair on this page is. If the burst pipe has already soaked flooring, drywall, or a finished basement, that structural cleanup and drying is a separate service; call our water damage restoration line for that part specifically, since it uses different equipment and follows a different process than the pipe repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first if a pipe bursts in my house?

Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely, then call our 24/7 emergency line. Turning off electricity near standing water and photographing the damage for insurance are the next steps while you wait.

Why do pipes burst more often in Chester during cold weather?

Many of Chester's older rowhomes and twins have exposed or under-insulated pipe runs in basements, crawlspaces, and exterior walls. A hard freeze can crack these lines, and the break often only becomes visible once the ice thaws.

Is a burst pipe covered by homeowners insurance?

Sudden, accidental water damage from a burst pipe is typically covered by standard homeowners insurance, while gradual leaks and normal wear usually are not. Check your specific policy for the details that apply to your claim.

Do you charge extra for emergency after-hours burst pipe calls?

No separate emergency surcharge structure is advertised; every call gets a flat price confirmed once a technician sees the damage, day or night.
