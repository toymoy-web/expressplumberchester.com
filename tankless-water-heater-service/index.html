No Hot Water From a Tankless Unit?

A tankless water heater failing doesn't look like a tank failing. There's no puddle at the base and no sudden flood, usually just an error code on the display, water that turns lukewarm partway through a shower, or the unit shutting off entirely when more than one fixture runs at once. Homeowners searching for a fix often don't yet know the term "tankless water heater service" covers exactly this.

The most common cause by far is mineral scale building up inside the heat exchanger, which is a normal maintenance issue rather than a sign the unit is failing, especially in homes that have never had the unit descaled since installation.

2

What Tankless Service Actually Involves

Descaling Flush

A vinegar or manufacturer-approved descaling solution is circulated through the heat exchanger to dissolve mineral buildup, typically restoring full flow and heat output.

Sensor & Igniter Diagnostics

An error code pointing to a flow sensor, flame sensor, or igniter is diagnosed against the manufacturer's code chart rather than guessed at, since tankless codes are specific.

Venting Check

Tankless units need different venting than tank units; a service call includes checking that venting still meets manufacturer spec, especially after any nearby construction or roofing work.

Signs Your Tankless Unit Needs Service

  • An error code displayed on the unit's control panel.
  • Water that starts hot then fades to lukewarm during use.
  • The unit shutting off when two fixtures run hot water at the same time.
  • A delay of several seconds longer than usual before hot water arrives.
  • Unusual popping or ticking sounds during operation.

How Often Descaling Is Actually Needed

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling in areas with moderate to hard water, and Chester's municipal water falls into that range. A unit that has gone several years without descaling is the single most common cause of a "failing" tankless heater that turns out to need nothing more than a flush. Widener University's campus sits in the middle of this same older housing stock, built in the same early-1900s wave.

Homes that skip descaling for many years risk permanent heat exchanger damage, at which point the fix moves from a $200 service call to a full unit replacement, which is the main reason this maintenance is worth staying ahead of rather than waiting for symptoms.

Repair, Descale, or Replace

A unit under 10 years old with a sensor or igniter issue is almost always repaired. A unit that has never been descaled and is showing flow or heat symptoms usually just needs that maintenance done. A unit with a damaged heat exchanger from years of untreated scale, or one that's simply reached the end of its service life, gets replaced; our water heater repair team handles both tank and tankless replacement.

Gas vs. Electric Tankless Units

A gas tankless unit needs a larger gas line than many older Chester homes originally had installed for a standard tank water heater, since instant heating draws more gas volume in a short burst than a tank does. That gas line sizing gets checked before a gas tankless install, not assumed to already be adequate.

Electric tankless units avoid the gas line question entirely but usually need a dedicated high-amperage circuit, which can mean an electrical panel upgrade in an older home with limited spare capacity. Both paths are workable; which one fits depends on what's already in place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my tankless water heater show an error code?

Error codes usually point to a specific cause: a flow sensor, an igniter, or a flame sensor issue, or a venting problem. The code on the display tells a technician where to start rather than needing to guess.

How often should a tankless water heater be descaled?

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling in areas with moderate to hard water, which includes Chester. A unit that has gone several years without descaling is the most common cause of reduced hot water output.

Is it worth switching from a tank to a tankless water heater?

Tankless units cost more upfront but provide continuous hot water and typically last longer than a tank unit with proper maintenance. Whether it's worth it depends on household hot water demand and existing gas line or electrical capacity, both worth discussing before switching.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency tankless water heater repair?

Yes, particularly for complete loss of hot water, which is treated as an emergency call on our 24/7 line.
