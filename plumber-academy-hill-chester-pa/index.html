Where Academy Hill Sits in Chester

Academy Hill is a neighborhood of the City of Chester according to City-Data, whose profile lists ZIP code 19013 and Walk Score also lists Academy Hill among Chester neighborhoods. The profile names State Route 320 (Providence Ave, Upland St and Madison St), State Route 352 (Edgmont Ave and Avenue of the States), I-95 and many streets from E 12th St through E 19th St, plus Melrose Ave, Walnut St, Chestnut St and Potter St. No official boundary is published, so we use these only as a guide.

The profile's build-year chart is unusual for Chester. It shows homes from nearly every period, from 1939 or earlier to 2020 or later, with sizable groups from before 1940, the 1940s, the 1970s and the 2000s. Attached homes make up about half of units and detached houses about a tenth. These are data-site estimates and not city records, and the profile figures for the area do not match the Walk Score population, so we do not quote a population.

Several streets in the profile, including Hancock St and Johnson St, also appear in the Sun Village and Sun Hill profiles. We treat the neighborhoods as neighbors with fuzzy edges.

Academy Hill is listed under Chester ZIP 19013. You can also see our main Chester plumbing page and our page for nearby Upland.

Plumbing Notes for Mixed-Age Housing

This section is general advice. When a neighborhood has homes from several eras, the equipment varies. A newer house may have a tankless or high-efficiency water heater and plastic supply lines. An older one may have had parts replaced over the years. We confirm what is installed before suggesting a repair.

If you are not sure of your home's age or its water heater, give us the address and the label details and we will take it from there.

  • Note whether the problem affects one fixture or the whole house.
  • Check the water heater for water at its base or an error light.
  • Test whether the main shutoff valve turns freely.
  • Avoid chemical drain openers if the drain is fully blocked, because they can splash back on whoever opens the line.
