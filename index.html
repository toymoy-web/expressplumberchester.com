What to Check Before You Call

A few quick checks can limit water damage while you wait for a plumber, especially in Chester's older rowhomes and twins where shutoff valves are not always easy to find on the first try.

  • Know where your home's main water shutoff valve is before an emergency happens; in older Chester rowhomes it is often in the basement or a front crawlspace, not under a sink.
  • If a pipe bursts, shut off the main valve first, then unplug nearby electronics or appliances if water is pooling near an outlet.
  • For a suspected gas smell near a water heater or gas line, leave the house and call from outside; do not flip light switches on the way out.
  • Take a few photos of standing water or damage before cleanup starts; this helps if you file a homeowners insurance claim for sudden water damage.
  • Turn off the water heater's power or gas supply if it is actively leaking, to reduce the chance of damage to the unit itself.
