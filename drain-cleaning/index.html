Kitchen, Bathroom, and Main Line Clogs

Drain calls in Chester split into two real categories with different fixes. A single fixture clog, a kitchen sink, tub, or one toilet, usually clears with a standard cable run.

A clog that backs up multiple fixtures at once, or comes back within a few weeks of a prior cleaning, points to a main line problem further down the system, often at a point where an older clay or cast iron lateral has settled or been invaded by tree roots. Rowhomes along Lamokin Street and other blocks dating to Chester's early-1900s industrial boom are typical examples.

Grease buildup in kitchen lines and hair or soap buildup in bathroom lines are the two most common single-fixture causes we see. A main line that clogs repeatedly is a different problem and usually needs a camera inspection rather than another cable pass.

Cabling vs. Hydro Jetting

Drain Snaking (Cabling)

A rotating cable clears the immediate blockage in a single fixture line or a short run, the right first step for most standalone clogs.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scours the full diameter of the pipe, removing grease buildup and root intrusion that cabling alone leaves behind, and is the standard fix for a main line that clogs repeatedly.

Camera Inspection

For a recurring or main-line clog, a camera confirms the actual cause, root intrusion, a cracked joint, or a low spot, before recommending jetting or a repair.

Emergency Signs Your Clog Is a Main Line Problem

  • More than one drain backs up at the same time (toilet and tub, for example).
  • Gurgling from a drain when a different fixture is used.
  • A clog that returns within a few weeks of being cleared.
  • Sewage odor near a floor drain or basement cleanout.
  • Water backing up into a lower-level fixture when the washing machine drains.

Older Plumbing Systems Need a Different Approach

A lot of drain calls in Chester come from homes with the original cast iron or clay drain lines still in place, sometimes shared between adjoining rowhome units. Scale buildup inside decades-old cast iron narrows the pipe's usable diameter over time, which means a line that cleared easily five years ago can clog faster today even with no change in household habits.

That is part of why a technician checks pipe material and age, not just the clog itself, before recommending cabling versus jetting. A cast iron line with heavy internal scale often responds better to jetting on the first visit instead of repeated cable calls that only buy a few weeks each time.

Kitchen lines get a separate note: grease that solidifies inside an older, narrower cast iron kitchen drain builds up faster than in a modern PVC line of the same size, which is a real reason repeat kitchen clogs are common in older Chester units even with normal cooking habits.

Shared lateral lines between adjoining rowhome units are another wrinkle worth flagging: a clog that looks like it belongs to one unit sometimes actually sits in a section shared with the property next door, which changes who is responsible for the fix and is worth confirming before repair work starts.

When a Clog Is an Emergency

A clog is an emergency when water is rising toward the rim, backing out of a floor drain, or showing up in more than one fixture. Stop running water and call our 24/7 line. A single slow sink can wait for a scheduled visit, but a backing-up main cannot.

We clear the stoppage, run a camera if the clog is deep, and tell you whether it will come back. For repeat clogs, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall instead of just boring a hole through the clog.

Kitchen Sink and Snaking Calls

Kitchen sink clogs are mostly grease and food waste, and they are usually cleared with a cable in one visit. Bathroom clogs are more often hair and soap. If water from a clogged drain also shows up elsewhere, the problem is lower in the system and the sewer line should be checked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my drain clog again a few weeks after being cleared?

A clog that returns quickly usually means the cable cleared the immediate blockage but not the underlying cause, often root intrusion or scale buildup in a main line. Hydro jetting or a camera inspection typically finds and fixes the real issue.

What is the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?

Snaking clears an immediate blockage with a rotating cable. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire pipe wall, removing buildup that snaking leaves behind, and is the standard fix for a recurring clog.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency drain cleaning in Chester?

Yes. A backed-up drain, especially one affecting more than one fixture, is treated as an emergency call on our 24/7 line.

How much does drain cleaning cost?

Standard snaking runs $150 to $400. Hydro jetting for a main line runs $350 to $800, confirmed after a technician sees the clog.

Is a backed-up drain an emergency?

It is when water is rising, backing out of floor drains, or affecting more than one fixture. Call (610) 672-7601 any hour.
