No Hot Water, a Clogged Drain, or a Leak in Boothwyn?

Whatever the exact plumbing problem, most calls from Boothwyn and the rest of Upper Chichester Township come down to a small set of real issues: a water heater going out, a drain that won't clear, or a leak that shows up as a higher water bill before it shows up as visible water. Upper Chichester is a large township, roughly 16,900 residents across 6.7 square miles, so where in the township you're located changes what's most likely: the older section around the Chichester Friends Meetinghouse and Booth's Corner has more mid-1900s housing, while newer development sits closer to the township's edges.

Booth's Corner, the Amish market at the center of Boothwyn, and the Chichester Friends Meetinghouse (built 1769, on the National Register of Historic Places) both sit within the township's older core, a useful landmark reference for confirming service coverage when scheduling a visit.

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

  • Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Turn off electricity to any area with standing water before touching outlets or appliances there.
  • Take a few photos of the damage before cleanup starts, for a homeowners insurance claim.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line; a technician will confirm arrival timing on the phone.

Signs You Need a Plumber Now

  • A water heater that's stopped producing hot water or is leaking at the base.
  • A drain that backs up repeatedly despite being cleared before.
  • A water bill that's risen with no change in usage.
  • A running or leaking toilet.
  • Low pressure at one fixture while the rest of the house is normal.
